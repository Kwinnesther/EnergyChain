# EnergyChain

A decentralized renewable energy trading platform with carbon credit rewards on Stacks blockchain.

## Features

- Renewable energy production registration and tracking
- Carbon credit calculation based on energy generation
- Grid operator management for energy distribution
- Automated credit rewards for clean energy producers
- Transparent energy reserve monitoring

## Smart Contract Functions

### Public Functions
- `activate-grid` - Initialize energy trading platform
- `register-production` - Register renewable energy production
- `calculate-carbon-credits` - Calculate carbon credits (operator only)
- `trade-energy-credits` - Trade energy and claim credits

### Read-Only Functions
- `get-producer-capacity` - Get producer's energy capacity
- `get-grid-stats` - Get platform statistics
- `get-energy-reserve` - Get total energy reserve

## Usage

Deploy the contract to create a decentralized energy trading platform where renewable energy producers can register their capacity and earn carbon credits based on their contribution to the grid.
